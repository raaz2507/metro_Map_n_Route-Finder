## 📜 Attribution Policy
All external 3D assets used in this project must adhere strictly to:
1. **CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain)**: Free for personal and commercial use without mandatory attribution.
2. **CC-BY 4.0 (Creative Commons Attribution)**: Free to use, adapt, and share, provided clear credit and source links are maintained in this document and the application's credits view.
3. **100% Free of Copyright Encroachment**: Zero proprietary, copyrighted, or unauthorized game rips.
