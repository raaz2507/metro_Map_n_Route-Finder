# 💳 <span style="color:#2563eb;">Metro Card Discount</span>

Travelling with a Smart Card is cheaper than buying tokens:

- ✅ **10% off** the token fare on every journey.
- ✅ **An extra 10% off** during off-peak hours.

---

## ⏰ <span style="color:#16a34a;">Off-Peak Hours (Extra Discount)</span>

- 🌅 **From start of service until 8:00 AM**
- ☀️ **12:00 PM to 5:00 PM**
- 🌙 **9:00 PM until end of service**

> ℹ️ Discounts apply automatically when you tap in with a Smart Card.  
> Fares shown in this app already reflect smart-card pricing.

---

# 🎫 <span style="color:#dc2626;">What the Fares Mean</span>

### 🪙 Token

A single-journey plastic token bought at the station for one trip.

- Full fare on **Monday–Saturday**
- Cheaper on **Sundays and National Holidays**

---

### 💳 Smart Card

A rechargeable card.

- Every journey is about **10% cheaper** than a token.
- Discount is applied automatically when you tap in.

---

### 🕒 Off-Peak Smart

The Smart Card fare during off-peak hours.

- Includes the regular **10% Smart Card discount**
- Earns an **additional 10% discount** during off-peak hours

---
