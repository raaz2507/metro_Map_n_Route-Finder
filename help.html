Route Planning & Navigation

Finding optimal transit paths, stations, and interchange guides

How to Search Stations & Plan a Route

Fuzzy search, station codes, and recent search history

YatraMarg features an intelligent fuzzy search engine to make route selection seamless:

  • Type station name, landmark, or 2-letter acronym (e.g. 'RC' for Rajiv Chowk).
  • Recent searches are saved locally on your device for instant 1-tap reselection.
  • Tap the swap icon (⇄) between Start and End stations to reverse your journey path immediately.

Shortest Route vs. Minimum Interchange

Understanding route optimization preferences

You can toggle routing priority based on your travel preferences:

  • Shortest Route: Finds the quickest path with lowest travel time, even if it requires an extra interchange.
  • Minimum Interchange: Prioritizes single-line direct journeys or least train transfers for comfort.
  • Interactive path highlighting displays intermediate stations and line switch colors on the map.

How Interchanges & Platform Transfers Work

Walking time, transfer bridges, and signage

When changing lines at major interchange hubs:

  • Our route timeline displays transfer walking time (typically 3 to 6 minutes) calculated in the total journey.
  • Follow color-coded foot-trails on station platforms matching your next metro line.
  • Stations like Rajiv Chowk, Kashmere Gate, and Hauz Khas have multi-level platform lifts and escalators.

Smart Journey & Sensors

Destination wake-up alarms, GPS tracking, and card recharge gateway

Destination Wake-Up Alarm (How it Works)

Geo-fenced sound & vibration alert before your stop

Never miss your destination station while reading, listening to music, or napping:

  • Set an alert radius (e.g., 1 or 2 stations before arrival) from the floating alarm button.
  • Uses low-power background Geolocation API and motion telemetry to track proximity.
  • Sounds a prominent audio chime and activates device vibration upon approaching the target station.

Live GPS, Speedometer & Sensor Permissions

Why permissions are requested and battery optimization

YatraMarg utilizes device hardware strictly client-side with zero tracking server uploads:

  • Location Permission (High Accuracy): Computes live train speed (km/h) and station arrival estimation.
  • Accelerometer / Motion: Automatically pauses telemetry when stationary to save up to 80% battery.
  • All telemetry data is strictly processed inside your browser sandbox and never sent to any server.

Smart Card Recharge Gateway

Fast online top-up via official portals

Top up your metro smart card directly within the application:

  • Click 'Recharge Card' in the top navigation bar to open the recharge dialog.
  • Input your 8 to 11 digit Smart Card engraved number and choose your preferred recharge amount.
  • Redirects securely to official transit recharge partners (DMRC / Paytm / Amazon Pay / Metro Web).

Fares, Smart Cards & Station Rules

Discounts, penalty rules, overstay limits, and holiday fare slabs

Smart Card Discounts & Peak vs Non-Peak Fares

Save 10% to 20% on every ride

Smart card holders receive standard tiered transit discounts:

  • Flat 10% discount on all journeys paid using a valid Metro Smart Card.
  • Additional 10% discount (Total 20%) during non-peak hours (e.g. before 08:00 AM, 12:00 PM to 05:00 PM, after 09:00 PM).
  • Special discounted fare slabs apply on Sundays and National Holidays.

Station Overstay & Maximum Time Limit Rules

Avoid automatic AFC gate penalty deduction

Metro systems impose maximum duration limits inside the paid area:

  • Same Station Entry/Exit: Maximum 20 minutes allowed (nominal minimum fare applicable).
  • Different Station (Under 10 km): Maximum 65 minutes inside transit system.
  • Long Journeys (> 10 km): Maximum 180 minutes. Exceeding limits incurs a standard hourly fine at AFC exit gates.

Baggage Dimensions & Weight Allowance

Permissible personal luggage standards

Passenger baggage regulations for smooth CISF screening:

  • Maximum weight: Up to 25 kg of personal luggage allowed per commuter.
  • Maximum dimensions: 80 cm (length) × 100 cm (width) × 60 cm (height).
  • Airport Express Line commuters are permitted up to 32 kg depending on airline check-in baggage policies.

App Settings & Troubleshooting

Offline mode, location fix, theme personalization, and cache reset

Using YatraMarg in Offline Mode (No Internet)

Cached network schematics and offline route calculation

You can find routes and view maps even underground without internet connection:

  • Once loaded, station network coordinates and route graphs are cached locally in your browser (CacheStorage).
  • The vector SVG interactive map runs 100% client-side without pinging external tile servers.
  • To refresh latest station additions, simply reload the app when an active internet connection is available.

Fixing GPS Location Errors Underground

Overcoming cellular signal loss in underground metro tunnels

Satellite GPS signals naturally weaken when traveling in deep underground tunnels:

  • Ensure 'Location Services' and 'High Accuracy Mode' are turned on in your device settings.
  • Near above-ground elevated stations, GPS locks back in within 5 to 10 seconds.
  • If GPS remains unavailable underground, use the 'Manual Station Check' feature in the journey tracker.

Switching Themes & App Language

Personalize your visual experience & native language

Customize YatraMarg to suit your preference in bright sunlight or nighttime travel:

  • Theme Selector in Header: Switch between Classic Light, Sleek Dark, Neon Cyberpunk, Vintage Retro, Forest Mint, and Ghibli.
  • Language Selector: Switch instantly between English and हिन्दी. All route labels, fares, and guides translate on the fly.
  • Your preferred theme and language are saved permanently in your browser localStorage.

How to Clear Cache & Reload Latest Data

Fixing stale maps or outdated station data

If you notice missing newly inaugurated stations or mismatched fares:

  • Hard refresh your browser by pressing Ctrl + F5 (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + R (Mac).
  • On mobile devices, open browser settings -> Site Settings -> Clear Data for this site.
  • New data updates are checked automatically by the Service Worker on every initial app visit.
