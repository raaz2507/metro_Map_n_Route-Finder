Core Architecture

100% Client-Side Privacy & Zero Tracking

Unlike standard commercial apps that log your daily travel habits, location traces, and commute routes to remote ad-servers, YatraMarg executes all graph algorithms, shortest-path computations, and sensor telemetry directly in your browser sandbox.

  • No user accounts, no login required, zero tracking cookies.
  • Geolocation & motion sensors operate 100% in-device without server pings.
High Reliability

Offline-First Engine

Deep underground tunnels often have zero cellular signal. YatraMarg caches network topologies, station coordinates, and route graphs locally so your route finder and vector SVG maps remain fully operational anywhere.

  • Browser CacheStorage & client-side graph execution.
  • Vector SVG schematics scale losslessly at any device zoom.
Federated Transit

Unified Multi-City Hub

Seamlessly switch between Delhi NCR (DMRC, NMRC, Rapid Metro, RRTS), Mumbai Metro, and expanding regional transit corridors under one cohesive, uniform interface.

  • Instant network switching without reloading separate apps.
  • Harmonized interchange transfer times and line codes.
Hardware Telemetry

Smart Travel Telemetry

Features like the Destination Wake-Up Alarm and live train speedometer leverage device hardware gracefully, automatically pausing when stationary to conserve battery.

  • Proximity alerts before your destination stop.
  • Battery-friendly sensor lifecycle management.

Statutory Transparency & Data Ethics

YatraMarg is an independent public digital initiative developed for commuters. It is not owned by, affiliated with, or an official entity of DMRC, NMRC, NCRTC, MMRDA, or any state transit corporation.

All transit routes, station names, and fare estimations are synchronized with official public schedules for informative assistance. For official ticket booking, smart card issuance, or legal transit matters, commuters are guided to the respective official transit authority portals via our Passenger Support directory.
